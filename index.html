Permissions Used

  • Camera: required for AR gameplay, body tracking, and optional "Try face" (iPhone-only; TrueDepth on iPhone X or newer).
  • Photos (Add Only): used only when saving recordings.
  • Activity timeline: minutes and strikes stored on your device only — not sent to Apple Health.
  • ARcade Arena is iPhone-only — not supported on iPad.
